# Game Overview

## What is GeoAIGames?

GeoAIGames is a geography learning game. It is played mainly as a **Telegram Mini App**: a small app that opens inside Telegram. The name is also written "Geo AI Games".

The game started as **Atlas Quest**, a ten-round quiz about country shapes. Atlas Quest is now **Level 1 · Atlas Explorer** of a five-level game. Levels 2 to 5 cover water, landforms, natural resources and mixed geography topics.

## Game concept and objectives

- **Learn by playing.** After every answer in Levels 2–5, the game shows a short explanation and names its source.
- **Answer on a real map.** Many questions are answered by tapping countries on a world map.
- **Collect points to progress.** Points from every game add up to a lifetime score. Higher scores unlock higher levels, and higher levels multiply their points (×1 to ×5).
- **Verified facts.** Questions are built from a reviewed knowledge base. Every fact has a source. Statistics state their year and whether they describe production, reserves or resources.

## Target audience

- **Primary:** Telegram users, from curious teenagers to adults, who want a short geography game they can play in a few minutes.
- **Secondary:** students and teachers who want sourced geography facts in a playful format.

## Main gameplay concept

Every game has **10 rounds**. In each round the player answers one question. A correct answer earns points; consecutive correct answers add a small streak bonus. When the game ends, the result is saved to the player's profile and added to their lifetime score.

| Level | Name | Theme | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Atlas Explorer | Countries: find them on the map and name them by their shape | ×1 |
| 2 | Hydro Explorer | Rivers, lakes, waterfalls, seas, oceans, straits and canals | ×2 |
| 3 | Earth Explorer | Mountain ranges, peaks, passes, valleys, volcanoes, canyons, tunnels | ×3 |
| 4 | Resource Explorer | Minerals and deposits; production versus reserves | ×4 |
| 5 | Master Explorer | Climate, regions, population, trade, shipping routes and combined facts | ×5 |

Details: [Levels and Stages](levels-guide.md).

## Supported platforms

| Platform | What works |
|---|---|
| Telegram (Mini App) | The full game: sign-in, all five levels, saved progress. Open `t.me/geoaigames_bot/play`. |
| A regular web browser (outside Telegram) | Level 1 only. There is no sign-in, so nothing is saved to a profile; best scores are kept on the device. Levels 2–5 show "Open GeoAIGames in Telegram to unlock". |

- The interface is mobile first and also works with a mouse on desktop.
- The interface language is English.
- A separate, older **Web Game** also exists. It is not part of the five levels and does not save progress to a profile. See [Game Features](game-features.md#web-game).

## General gameplay flow

1. **Open** the Mini App from Telegram. The app signs the player in automatically with their Telegram account.
2. **Level Selection** (home screen) shows the player's name, lifetime score and player level, and five level cards.
3. **Choose a level.** Locked levels show how many points are still needed.
4. **Introduction screen.** Level 1: choose Easy or Hard, then Play. Levels 2–5: read the categories and rules, then Start game.
5. **Play 10 rounds.** After each answer the correct answer is shown.
6. **Result screen:** correct answers, points, best streak, best score, and any level that was just unlocked. The player can play again, challenge a friend or return to the menu.

## Terminology

These terms are used with the same meaning on every page.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Game (session) | One play-through of a level: 10 rounds from start to result screen. |
| Round | One question inside a game. |
| Level | One of the five themed parts of the game (Level 1 to Level 5). |
| Mode | A variant of a level. Level 1 has two modes, Easy and Hard. Levels 2–5 have one mode each. |
| Base score | Points before the level multiplier is applied. |
| Level multiplier | The factor (×1 to ×5) applied to the base score of a level. |
| Streak | The number of consecutive fully correct answers in the current game. |
| Streak bonus | Extra points for a correct answer that continues a streak. |
| Lifetime score | The sum of the scores of all saved games of a player, in all levels. Also called "total score" and shown as "Score". |
| Player level | The highest level up to which every level is unlocked. Shown as "Level" next to the player's name. |
| Best score | The highest score of a single game, kept per mode and per level. |
| Mastery | For Levels 2–5: how many of the level's questions the player has answered fully correctly at least once. |
| Unlock | Making a level playable. Unlocks are permanent. |
