# FAQ

## Playing

### How do I start playing?

Open `t.me/geoaigames_bot/play` in Telegram. The game signs you in with your Telegram account automatically. Tap the Level 1 card, choose Easy or Hard, and tap Play. See [Game Rules and Conditions](game-rules.md#how-to-start-a-game).

### Do I need to create an account?

No. Your Telegram account is your GeoAIGames account. There is no separate registration, password or e-mail.

### How can I connect my Telegram account?

You don't need to do anything. Opening the Mini App inside Telegram connects your Telegram account automatically. The first time you open it, your profile is created.

### Can I play without Telegram?

Yes, but only Level 1. In a regular web browser there is no sign-in, so your progress is not saved to a profile and Levels 2–5 stay locked. Best scores are kept on that device.

### How many rounds does a game have?

Always 10 rounds.

### Is there a time limit?

No. You can take as long as you need for each answer. There is no time bonus either.

### Can I change my answer?

No. Each round can be answered once.

### What is the difference between Easy and Hard in Level 1?

Easy uses the 75 largest countries and a wider map view. Hard uses all countries of the quiz, more look-alike neighbours and a tighter map view. Hard gives twice the points: 20 per correct answer instead of 10, and a streak bonus of up to 10 instead of 5.

### How do I zoom the map?

Pinch with two fingers. In Level 1, two fingers zoom and move the map, and one finger only taps. In Levels 2–5 you can also drag with one finger, use the mouse wheel on a computer, and tap ⤢ to return to the question's view. See [Game Features](game-features.md#zoom-and-touch-controls).

### A country is too small to tap. What can I do?

Zoom in with two fingers. Very small countries (micro-states) cannot be tapped on the world map at all. When one of them is part of a correct answer, the app names it after you answer.

### What happens if I leave a game in the middle?

The game is abandoned and earns no points. Pressing Telegram's Back button during a game, or starting another game, abandons the current one.

## Score and levels

### How are points calculated?

A correct answer is worth 10 points (20 in Level 1 Hard), plus a streak bonus for consecutive correct answers, multiplied by the level multiplier (×1 to ×5). A wrong answer is worth 0 points. See [Scoring System](scoring-system.md).

### What is the streak bonus?

Each correct answer in a row adds one more point, up to 5 extra points (Level 1 Hard: two more points, up to 10). A wrong or partly correct answer resets the streak.

### Why did I get only some of the points?

In questions with several correct answers or with pairs to match, you get points for the part you got right. Wrong picks reduce these points. Partial answers have no streak bonus. See [Scoring System](scoring-system.md#partial-answers-levels-25).

### How do I unlock the next level?

Collect lifetime points: 1,000 for Level 2, 3,000 for Level 3, 6,000 for Level 4 and 10,000 for Level 5. Points from every game in every level count. See [Levels and Stages](levels-guide.md#unlocking-levels).

### Do I have to complete a level to unlock the next one?

No. Only your lifetime score counts.

### Can a level become locked again?

No. Unlocks are permanent, and your player level never goes down.

### Where can I see my score?

Your lifetime score and player level are shown next to your name on the Level Selection screen, for example "Ana (Score: 1,095 | Level: 2)". Each level card shows your best score. The result screen shows the score of the game you just played.

### Why did my old scores become smaller?

Version 2.1.0 divided all point values by 10. Results from before version 2.1.0 count as one tenth of their original value. Your saved results were not changed; they are only counted on the new scale.

## Saving progress

### Why did my progress not update?

Check these causes:

1. **Not signed in.** Progress is saved only when the game runs inside Telegram and you are signed in. In a regular browser nothing is saved to a profile.
2. **The game was not finished.** Abandoned games earn no points.
3. **The result could not be saved.** If the result screen says "Couldn't save this game", tap Try again (Levels 2–5). In Level 1, a result that could not be saved is not added to your lifetime score.
4. **The session expired.** If the app says "Session expired", close the Mini App and open it again from the bot.

When a game is saved, the result screen shows "☁️ Saved to your profile" and your score next to your name is updated.

### Is my progress the same on all my devices?

Yes, if you play inside Telegram with the same Telegram account. Your lifetime score, unlocked levels, player level and best scores are stored in your profile on the GeoAIGames server.

### Can I reset or delete my profile?

Not from within the app in version 2.3.0.

## Other

### Who checks whether my answer is correct?

The GeoAIGames server, using a reviewed set of geographic facts. AI is never used to check answers.

### Where do the facts come from?

From named sources such as Natural Earth, Wikidata, Wikipedia, the U.S. Geological Survey and the World Bank. Every explanation names its source.

### Is there a leaderboard or achievements?

No. Leaderboards and achievements do not exist in version 2.3.0.

### In which languages is the game available?

English only, in version 2.3.0.
