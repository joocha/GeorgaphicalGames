## Releases before the documentation platform

These releases happened before public documentation existed. They are summarized from the project's developer changelog. Only changes that affect players are listed.

### 2.2.0 (2026-11-18)

- **88 new Hydro Explorer questions.** Level 2 grows from 22 to 110 questions: oceans (including currents and trench depths), lakes, rivers, straits, waterfalls, seas and canals.

### 2.1.0 (2026-11-18)

- **Points divided by 10.** A correct answer is worth 10 points (plus a streak bonus of up to 5) instead of 100 (plus up to 50).
- **Level 1 Hard pays double:** 20 points per correct answer plus a streak bonus of up to 10.
- **Levels unlock by lifetime score:** Level 2 at 1,000, Level 3 at 3,000, Level 4 at 6,000 and Level 5 at 10,000 points. Before, a level unlocked when the previous level had been completed once. Levels unlocked before this change stay unlocked.
- **Player level** shown next to the player's name: "Name (Score: … | Level: …)".
- Locked level cards show the points still needed.
- Results saved before this version count as one tenth of their stored value.

### 2.0.0 (2026-11-17)

- **Five-level game.** Atlas Quest becomes Level 1 · Atlas Explorer. New levels: Hydro Explorer, Earth Explorer, Resource Explorer and Master Explorer, with point multipliers ×1 to ×5.
- **Level Selection** home screen with level cards and introduction screens.
- **Question engine** for Levels 2–5: nine question types, answers on the map, partial points, explanations with sources.
- **Interactive map:** drag, pinch and wheel zoom in Levels 2–5, river and lake layers.
- Scores and unlocks are calculated by the server.

### 1.4.4 – 1.4.8 (2026-11-14 to 2026-11-16)

- **Telegram sign-in and saved progress.** Inside Telegram the game signs the player in, and game results are saved to the player's profile.
- Clearer sign-in messages ("Try again", "Session expired").

### 1.0 – 1.4 (2026-11-03 to 2026-11-12)

- First release of **Atlas Quest**, the country-shape quiz, as a Telegram Mini App and as a browser game.
- Server and database foundations for saving progress.
